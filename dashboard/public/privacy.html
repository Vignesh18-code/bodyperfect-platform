BodyPerfect Clinic — App Privacy Notice

Body Perfect Holistic Dieting & Weight Control Services L.L.C. operates BodyPerfect Clinic. This notice covers the Android/iOS app, clinic dashboard and supporting services. Contact info@bodyperfect.ae for privacy, correction, access or deletion requests. The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Information and purpose

We process your name, email address, phone number, account identifier and authentication information to manage and secure your account. Optional profile photos and preferences personalize your account. Appointments, clinic-assigned care instructions, treatment sessions, reports and clinic messages support your care and clinic administration. Reward and voucher records support the clinic's offers. Limited operational and security records help prevent misuse and maintain the service. Passwords are hashed; authentication codes expire.

Hosting and recipients

The active app backend, database, uploaded files, operational logs and recovery backups use Microsoft Azure UAE North. Authorized clinic personnel access information according to their assigned role and branch. We use Resend to deliver account verification and password-reset email. These messages contain the recipient email address, a temporary code and the BodyPerfect name, not medical reports or treatment details. Resend stores customer data, including email content and delivery logs, in the United States. Your recipient email provider may also process these messages outside the UAE. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

AI and clinic messaging

AI responses are disabled in this release's backend. New messages are not sent to OpenAI. Clinic-team messaging remains separate. Any future AI activation requires an updated notice, the applicable processing approvals and the app's explicit consent step. Older optional AI history may remain until cleared or expired; exchanges expire after 90 days and usage-limit metadata after 48 hours.

Retention

For verified and approved account-deletion requests, non-clinical account details, optional photos, rewards, AI history and administrative messages approved for deletion are removed within 30 days. Login access and sessions are revoked during fulfillment. The clinic reviews messages individually: clinically relevant information is retained under the healthcare schedule.

Medical records are generally retained for at least 25 years after the last clinical encounter under the applicable DHA policy. Some categories require longer: genetic records may require 30 years, and certain medico-legal records and patient indexes require indefinite retention. The responsible clinic reviewer must record the applicable category, period and any legal hold. There is no automatic clinical-record erasure when an app account closes. Retained identifying details are restricted and encrypted separately from the closed login account.

Routine Azure operational logs have a 30-day retention setting. Clinical-access and deletion audit records follow the applicable healthcare schedule. Database recovery backups and daily uploaded-file snapshots have a 30-day rolling retention setting. Backup recovery must reapply account-deletion decisions before restoring user access. Backups are not the clinic's long-term medical archive.

Your choices and account deletion

In the app, open Profile → Account privacy to request deletion using your password. You may also email info@bodyperfect.ae with the subject “BodyPerfect account deletion” and your account email. Do not include passwords or medical records. The clinic verifies your identity, reviews the request and explains any retained categories and periods. You can request access, correction or deletion of particular data without deleting the account by emailing info@bodyperfect.ae with the subject “BodyPerfect data request”, your account email and the category concerned, such as your profile photo or administrative messages. The clinic verifies your identity, removes eligible non-clinical data within 30 days of approval, and explains any clinical or legal retention that applies. Submitting a request does not immediately erase records.

Device permissions and external services

Photo selection is optional. Directions and telephone actions open your device's maps or calling app and are subject to those providers' settings and notices. The app does not need continuous location tracking to display clinic directions.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice when processing changes. Questions and complaints may be sent to info@bodyperfect.ae. Medical or urgent-care questions should be directed to the clinic, not privacy email.

Migration and recovery copies

Restricted legacy migration and encrypted local recovery copies remain subject to the clinic’s approved retention and retirement process. These copies are not used to serve the active app. Access is limited to authorized recovery personnel.
