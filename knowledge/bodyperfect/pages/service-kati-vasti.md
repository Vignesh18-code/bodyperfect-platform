Relieve Lower Back Pain Naturally with Ayurvedic Kati Vasti Therapy
Kati Vasti is a traditional Ayurvedic therapy that targets the lower back region to alleviate pain, stiffness, and discomfort. By pooling warm medicated oil on the lumbar area, it deeply nourishes spinal tissues, reduces inflammation, and supports the healing of chronic back problems. This holistic treatment is ideal for anyone seeking safe and natural relief from lower back issues.
How Kati Vasti Works
A dough ring is created using black gram flour and placed over the lower back (lumbar region). Warm herbal oil is poured and retained in this space for a specific time. The oil’s warmth penetrates the muscles and spine, promoting deep relaxation, easing nerve compression, and enhancing tissue repair.

FAQ
What conditions can Kati Vasti help treat?
Kati Vasti is effective for sciatica, herniated discs, lumbago, and general lower back stiffness.
How is the treatment performed?
A dough well is placed on the lower back and filled with warm medicated oil, which is retained for 30–40 minutes.
Is the treatment painful?
No, it is a relaxing and warming therapy that eases tension and improves circulation.
How many sessions are required?
Typically, a series of 5 to 7 sessions provides noticeable and lasting results.
Can Kati Vasti prevent back surgery?
In many cases, yes—it can reduce symptoms to a level that surgery may be postponed or avoided altogether.
