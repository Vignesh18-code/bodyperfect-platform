How Electro Stimulation Works
Electro Stimulation, also known as EMS (Electrical Muscle Stimulation), uses low-frequency electrical pulses to contract muscles, mimicking the natural process during exercise. This stimulates deep muscle fibers, enhancing strength, tone, and endurance—even in areas that are hard to target with traditional workouts.
What to Expect Before, During & After Treatment:
Before the session, a consultation will identify your goals and target areas. During treatment, electrode pads are placed on the skin, delivering gentle electrical pulses. You’ll feel a rhythmic tightening of the muscles—similar to an intense workout. After the session, there may be mild soreness, like post-exercise muscle fatigue, but there’s no downtime.

Does EMS help with weight loss?
While EMS primarily builds and tones muscle, it also burns calories and can support fat loss with regular use.
How long is an EMS session?
Sessions typically last 20 to 30 minutes, depending on the area and intensity level.
