Rejuvenate Muscles and Joints with the Healing Power of Kizhi Therapy
Kizhi is a revitalizing Ayurvedic therapy that uses warm herbal poultices to massage the body, easing muscle tension, improving circulation, and relieving joint stiffness. Ideal for those suffering from arthritis, sports injuries, or general body pain, Kizhi provides deep tissue relaxation while detoxifying and strengthening the body naturally.
How Kizhi Therapy Works
In Kizhi, medicated herbs are tied into cloth boluses, heated in herbal oils, and gently pounded or massaged over the affected areas. The warmth and herbal essence penetrate deep into tissues, enhancing blood flow, reducing inflammation, and soothing muscle and joint pain.

FAQ
What are the types of Kizhi used in treatment?
Kizhi can be customized as Ela Kizhi (leaf), Podi Kizhi (powder), or Navara Kizhi (rice), depending on your condition.
Is the therapy painful or uncomfortable?
Not at all—the warmth is soothing, and the massage is gentle yet deeply relaxing.
How long does a Kizhi session last?
A session typically lasts 30–45 minutes, depending on the treatment area and condition.
Can Kizhi help with chronic conditions?
Yes, it is especially effective for long-term joint and muscle issues like arthritis, spondylitis, and fibromyalgia.
How many sessions are needed?
While you may feel relief after the first session, a full course of 7–10 sessions offers the best long-term results.
