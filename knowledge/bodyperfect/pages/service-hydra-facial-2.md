What is a Hydrafacial Treatment?
Hydrafacial is a medical-grade, non-invasive facial treatment that combines cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, hydration, and antioxidant infusion in one powerful session. Hydrafacial is suitable for all skin types and delivers immediate, visible results with zero downtime. How long does a HydraFacial session take?
A typical HydraFacial session lasts about 30–45 minutes, making it a quick, convenient treatment that fits into even the busiest schedules.
Is HydraFacial suitable for all skin types?
Yes, HydraFacial is safe and effective for all skin types and tones, including sensitive skin. It can be customized to address specific skin concerns.
