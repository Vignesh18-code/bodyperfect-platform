Madero Therapy is a natural, non-invasive body sculpting technique that uses specially designed wooden tools to target cellulite, reduce localized fat, and improve body contour. How Madero Therapy Works:

FAQ
Is Madero Therapy painful?
No, it’s generally a comfortable treatment. Most clients find it relaxing, similar to a firm massage.
How many sessions are needed?
While some results can be seen after the first few sessions, a series of 5–10 treatments is recommended for optimal results.
Are the results permanent?
Results are long-lasting with proper diet, hydration, and lifestyle, though maintenance sessions are encouraged.
Can anyone get Madero Therapy?
It’s suitable for most people, but it’s best to consult your provider if you’re pregnant or have underlying medical conditions.
What areas of the body can be treated?
Common areas include abdomen, thighs, hips, arms, and buttocks. Sessions can be customized based on your goals.
