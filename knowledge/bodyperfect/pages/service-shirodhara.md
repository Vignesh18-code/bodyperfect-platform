Achieve Deep Mental and Physical Relaxation with Shirodhara Therapy
Shirodhara is a traditional Ayurvedic treatment where a continuous stream of warm herbal oil is gently poured over the forehead, promoting deep relaxation and mental clarity. It is especially effective for stress, anxiety, insomnia, and nervous system disorders. Shirodhara calms the mind, balances doshas, and rejuvenates the body for overall wellness.
How Shirodhara Works
During Shirodhara, a special vessel slowly pours warm, medicated oil over the “third eye” area on the forehead. This steady flow stimulates the pituitary gland, relaxes the nervous system, and promotes the release of serotonin and other calming hormones. The therapy brings a meditative state and restores balance to the mind and body.

FAQ
What oils are used in Shirodhara?
Herbal oils like Ksheerabala, Brahmi, or medicated buttermilk are used, depending on your body type and condition.
How long does each session last?
A typical session takes about 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the therapist’s recommendation.
Is Shirodhara messy or difficult to clean up?
The oil is applied gently and therapists assist with post-treatment cleanup, including head massage and wiping.
How many sessions are needed?
You may feel benefits after one session, but 5–7 sessions are ideal for deeper mental and emotional relief.
Can Shirodhara be done at home?
While possible, it’s best done under expert supervision to ensure proper technique, oil temperature, and environment.
