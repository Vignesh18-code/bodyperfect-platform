When you interact with our website, we may collect the following types of information:
Personal Details: Name, email address, phone number, delivery address
Account Information: Login credentials when you register on our website
Payment Details: Processed securely through our payment gateway (e.g., Telr)
Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and browsing behavior on our site
2. How We Use Your Information
Process and manage your orders
Create and manage your user account
Communicate with you regarding your orders or inquiries
Improve our website and user experience
Send promotional offers (only if you opt-in)
3. Payment Information
All payment transactions on BodyPerfect.ae are processed securely via our payment gateway partner Telr. We do not store your credit/debit card information on our servers.
View Telr’s privacy policy: https://telr.com/privacy-policy/
4. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:
With payment processors (like Telr) to complete your transaction
If required by law or legal authorities
5. Data Security
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
6. Your Rights
Access the personal data we hold about you
Request correction or deletion of your data
Withdraw consent for marketing communications
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: info@bodyperfect.ae
