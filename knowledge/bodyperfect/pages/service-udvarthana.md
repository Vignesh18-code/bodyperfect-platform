How Udvarthana Therapy Works
Udvarthana involves a rhythmic massage using dry or medicated herbal powders, often blended with warm oils. The coarse texture stimulates the skin and underlying tissues, activating metabolism and assisting in the elimination of toxins. The vigorous massage encourages blood flow and breaks down fat deposits, making it especially beneficial for those with Kapha dosha imbalance, sluggish metabolism, or fluid retention.
