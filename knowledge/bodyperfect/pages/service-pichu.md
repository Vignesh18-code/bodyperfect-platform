Targeted Relief and Relaxation with Ayurvedic Pichu Therapy
Pichu is a deeply soothing Ayurvedic treatment where a warm, medicated oil-soaked cotton pad is placed over the affected area—commonly the head, neck, spine, or joints. It’s highly effective for relieving localized pain, inflammation, stiffness, and neurological conditions. This therapy nourishes tissues and balances the doshas, especially Vata, promoting lasting comfort and healing.
How Pichu Therapy Works
In this therapy, a thick cotton pad is soaked in warm herbal oil and placed over the targeted area for a specific duration. The continuous warmth and oil absorption allow the medicine to deeply penetrate tissues, reducing inflammation, calming nerves, and restoring strength to joints and muscles.

FAQ
What conditions is Pichu used for?
Pichu is effective for neck pain, back pain, joint stiffness, migraines, frozen shoulder, and spinal issues.
Does the treatment cause any discomfort?
No, Pichu is a completely relaxing and soothing therapy with no discomfort or pain involved.
How long does a Pichu session take?
Each session typically lasts 20 to 40 minutes depending on the area being treated and the condition.
How many sessions are needed?
It varies per individual, but 5–7 sessions are common for lasting relief in chronic cases.
Can Pichu be combined with other treatments?
Yes, Pichu works well alongside therapies like Abhyanga, Kati Vasti, or Shirodhara for enhanced benefits.
