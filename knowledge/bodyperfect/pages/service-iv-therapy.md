FAQ
How often should I get IV Therapy?
Depending on your health goals, once a week or once a month is ideal. Your provider will guide you based on your needs.
Is the procedure painful?
You may feel a small pinch at insertion, but the drip is painless and most clients relax during the session.
Are there any side effects?
Side effects are rare, but minor bruising or light-headedness can occasionally occur. Most people feel great afterward.
Can I choose what goes in my IV?
Yes, IV drips are customizable. You can select blends for energy, immunity, hydration, skin glow, and more.
Who should consider IV Therapy?
Anyone needing fast hydration, energy, or nutrient support—athletes, busy professionals, or anyone under stress or fatigue.
