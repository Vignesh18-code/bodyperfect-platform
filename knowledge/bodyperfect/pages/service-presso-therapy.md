Revitalize Your Body with Advanced Pressotherapy Treatment
Pressotherapy is a non-invasive, therapeutic body treatment that uses air pressure massage to stimulate lymphatic drainage, reduce swelling, and improve circulation. Commonly used for detox, slimming, and recovery, Pressotherapy offers a relaxing and effective way to eliminate toxins, improve skin tone, and promote overall wellness.
How Pressotherapy Works:
During a Pressotherapy session, a specialized suit is placed on your legs, abdomen, or arms. This suit inflates and deflates in cycles, applying controlled pressure to promote lymphatic flow and blood circulation. It’s a painless, relaxing experience that helps flush out toxins, reduce fluid retention, and support fat metabolism.

FAQ
Is Pressotherapy safe?
Yes, it’s a clinically approved and non-invasive treatment that’s safe for most individuals.
How many sessions are needed?
While some feel better after one session, a series of 6–10 treatments provides the best long-term results.
What does the treatment feel like?
It feels like a gentle, rhythmic massage. Many people find it deeply relaxing and even fall asleep during the session.
Can it help with post-liposuction recovery?
Absolutely. Pressotherapy is often recommended to speed up healing, reduce swelling, and improve lymphatic drainage post-procedure.
Who should avoid Pressotherapy?
People with deep vein thrombosis, heart issues, or infections should consult a healthcare provider before trying Pressotherapy.
