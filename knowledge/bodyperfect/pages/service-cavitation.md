What is Ultrasound Cavitation Treatment?
Ultrasound Cavitation is a non-surgical body contouring procedure that targets and eliminates stubborn fat deposits using low-frequency ultrasound waves. During the treatment, these waves penetrate the skin and create micro-bubbles within the fat tissue, causing fat cells to break down without harming surrounding tissues.
The liquified fat is then naturally processed and eliminated by the body through the lymphatic system. Cavitation is ideal for reducing fat in common problem areas such as the abdomen, thighs, love handles, and upper arms. It is painless, safe, and does not require any downtime, making it a popular alternative to liposuction. While cavitation is not a weight-loss treatment, it is highly effective for inch loss, body sculpting, and improving skin texture. When combined with treatments like RF, it also helps in skin tightening post-fat reduction for optimal contouring results.

FAQ
How does ultrasound cavitation work?
It uses low-frequency ultrasound waves to break down fat cells, which are then naturally eliminated through the lymphatic system.
Is the procedure painful?
No, it’s completely painless. You may feel a slight warming or buzzing sensation during treatment.
How many sessions are needed?
Visible results are typically seen after 6–10 sessions, depending on the body area and fat density.
Is it safe?
Yes, it’s non-invasive and safe for most healthy adults with no major medical conditions.
Do I need to follow any aftercare?
Yes—drink plenty of water, avoid fatty foods, and stay active to help flush out broken fat cells effectively.
