What Are Exosomes?
Exosomes are tiny nano-sized vesicles naturally released by stem cells. These vesicles are packed with proteins, lipids, RNA, and growth factors that signal your cells to regenerate, repair, and optimize function. Unlike stem cells, exosomes do not replicate, making them a safe and efficient regenerative therapy with no risk of immune rejection.
