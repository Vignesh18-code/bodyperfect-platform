FAQ
Is Nasyam suitable for everyone?
It is generally safe but should be avoided during fever, menstruation, or pregnancy. Always consult an Ayurvedic expert first.
How often should I take Nasyam treatment?
Depending on your health, it may be taken daily for a short time or weekly/monthly as a part of maintenance therapy.
Does Nasyam have any side effects?
When done under expert supervision, Nasyam is safe and side-effect-free. Mild irritation is normal in the first session.
Can Nasyam help with migraines and stress?
Yes, it is especially effective for relieving migraines, stress, and anxiety by calming the head and nervous system.
What should I avoid after a Nasyam session?
Avoid exposure to cold air, direct sunlight, and strenuous activity for a few hours after the session.
