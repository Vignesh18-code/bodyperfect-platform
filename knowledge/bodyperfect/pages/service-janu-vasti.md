Soothe Knee Pain Naturally with Ayurvedic Janu Vasti Therapy
Janu Vasti is a powerful Ayurvedic therapy specifically designed to treat knee joint pain and stiffness. This traditional treatment involves retaining warm medicated oil on the knee area to deeply nourish tissues, reduce inflammation, and improve joint mobility. It’s a natural, non-invasive option for people with arthritis, ligament injuries, or age-related joint issues.
How Janu Vasti Works
A dough ring made of black gram flour is placed around the knee to create a reservoir. Warm, herbalized oil is gently poured into this space and allowed to sit for a set duration. The warmth of the oil penetrates deep into the joint, relieving pain, lubricating the cartilage, and supporting tissue repair from within.
